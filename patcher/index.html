Tag Hack Patcher

X-Men vs. Street Fighter (PlayStation, USA) as a real tag-team game

Pick your own disc image; this page builds the patched image on your computer and hands it back. Nothing is uploaded, and the page contains no game data.

1Your disc image

2Arcade ROM set optional

Adds animation frames the PlayStation port cut. They are used when the machine has 8 MB of RAM (DuckStation / SwanStation: Settings → Console → Enable 8MB RAM; on a MiSTer or a modified console, its 8 MB RAM option). The same image also runs on a stock 2 MB console, as the normal tag hack.

      Download .bin Download .cue

      Keep the .bin and the .cue together under the same name. They load in emulators as they are and can be converted to CHD with chdman createcd.

      Restored frames need 8 MB of RAM: in DuckStation / SwanStation switch on Settings → Console → Enable 8MB RAM; on a MiSTer or a modified console use its 8 MB RAM option. The main menu then shows “8MB” next to the version, and OPTION MODE has an ARCADE FRAMES ON / OFF line. Without it this image plays as the normal 2 MB tag hack.
